ABOUT

My name is Vicrina Aberdeen and I am a Sierra Leonean-American originally from Ohio. I am passionate about applying my blend of design and brand specialization to fashion, beauty, and lifestyle industries, aiming to foster brand loyalty and enrich user interactions. When I am not designing, I enjoy cooking a hearty meal from scratch, singing my heart out at a karaoke bar, trying a DIY art project and exploring a new city.
